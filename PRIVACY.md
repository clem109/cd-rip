# Privacy

CD Rip stores audio, artwork, lyrics and recovery records in your chosen archive folder.
It has no analytics, accounts, advertising or automatic update service.

Metadata requests go to MusicBrainz (disc ID or selected release), Cover Art Archive
(release identifiers, including redirects to its image hosts), and LRCLIB (artist,
album, track title and duration). Those services receive your IP address and normal
HTTP request information. Audio is not sent to these providers. Turn off lyrics in
Settings to stop lyric lookups; cached lyrics may still be embedded.

Music import adds local files to Apple's Music app. Any subsequent cloud syncing is
controlled by your Music settings, not CD Rip. Music Automation permission is used
only to add the selected finished files. Archive folder access is used to read and
write your collection.

Do not post job files or activity logs publicly without checking their local paths
and album information first.
